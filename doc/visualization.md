# Galactic Cosmological Orrery & Experience Horizons

## Overview

The SBX Spectroscopic Binaries database contains **5,169 orbits** across **4,080 stellar systems** and **224,148 radial velocity epochs**. To explore this dataset through an intuitive yet astrophysically rigorous cosmological perspective, we transform relational catalog parameters into a **3D Galactic Coordinate Space $(l, b, d)$** coupled with:
1. **Galactic Kinematic Velocity Flow** (Gaia proper motions & systemic radial velocities).
2. **In-Situ Animated Keplerian Mini-Orreries** with real-time eccentric anomaly solving and astrophysical spectral classification temperatures.
3. **Milky Way Galactic Context** with spiral arms, Sagittarius A* at $(X=8{,}200\text{ pc})$, and the 300 pc Local Bubble cavity.
4. **5-Tier Experience Horizon Model** for calibrating observational maturity and physical complexity.

The standalone interactive WebGL visualization is generated by [`cosmic_orrery.py`](../cosmic_orrery.py) and can be opened in any web browser at [`doc/cosmic_orrery.html`](cosmic_orrery.html).

## 1. Galactic Coordinate Transformation & Milky Way Context

Each binary system is mapped from equatorial celestial coordinates (Right Ascension $\alpha$, Declination $\delta$) into true Galactic coordinates $(l, b)$ using the IAU standard rotation matrix:

$$\sin b = \sin\delta \sin\delta_p + \cos\delta \cos\delta_p \cos(\alpha - \alpha_p)$$
$$\cos b \sin(l - l_0) = \cos\delta \sin(\alpha - \alpha_p)$$

Where $\alpha_p = 192.85948^\circ$, $\delta_p = 27.12825^\circ$ (Galactic North Pole), and $l_0 = 32.93192^\circ$.

### Galactic Cartesian Projection
$$\begin{aligned}
X &= d \cdot \cos(b) \cdot \cos(l) \quad (\text{toward Galactic Center, } l=0^\circ) \\
Y &= d \cdot \cos(b) \cdot \sin(l) \quad (\text{direction of Galactic Rotation, } l=90^\circ) \\
Z &= d \cdot \sin(b) \quad (\text{Galactic North Pole})
\end{aligned}$$

- **Galactic Center (Sagittarius A\*)**: Marked at $(X=8{,}200\text{ pc}, Y=0, Z=0)$.
- **Spiral Arms**: Logarithmic spiral arm models (Orion-Cygnus, Perseus, Sagittarius, Scutum-Centaurus).
- **Local Bubble Cavity**: 300 pc translucent spherical boundary depicting the interstellar cavity surrounding the Solar System.

## 2. Galactic Kinematics & Space Velocity Vector Flow

For systems with Gaia DR3 astrometry, 3D space velocity vectors are computed:
- Tangential velocities in km/s:
  $$v_\text{tan} = 4.74047 \times 10^{-3} \cdot \mu \cdot d$$
- Combined with systemic radial velocity $\gamma$:
  $$\mathbf{v} = (v_l, v_b, \gamma)$$
- Animated **velocity vector trails (comet tails)** project each system's forward space trajectory, highlighting coherent disk rotation versus high-velocity halo runaway pairs.

## 3. In-Situ Animated Keplerian Mini-Orreries

Clicking any star in the 3D scene opens the real-time **Keplerian Orbit Inspector**:
1. Solves **Kepler's Equation** numerically at 60 FPS:
   $$M = E - e \sin E$$
2. Models the primary and secondary orbital ellipses around the true center of mass (barycenter):
   $$a_1 = a \cdot \frac{M_2}{M_1 + M_2}, \quad a_2 = a \cdot \frac{M_1}{M_1 + M_2}$$
3. Displays **physical stellar colors** derived from Harvard spectral classifications:
   - **O / B**: Deep blue & blue-white ($\approx 15{,}000 - 30{,}000\text{ K}$)
   - **A / F**: White & pale yellow ($\approx 7{,}000 - 9{,}000\text{ K}$)
   - **G**: Solar yellow ($\approx 5{,}700\text{ K}$)
   - **K / M**: Orange & cool red ($\approx 3{,}200 - 4{,}500\text{ K}$)
   - **Dark Remnants / Pulsars**: Relativistic high-mass markers.

## 5. Controls & Interaction Guide

Open [`doc/cosmic_orrery.html`](cosmic_orrery.html) in your browser:

- **Tier Buttons**: Toggle experience horizons to filter systems by empirical maturity.
- **Velocity Flow Checkbox**: Toggle 3D proper motion and systemic velocity vector trails.
- **Spiral Arms & Local Bubble**: Toggle Milky Way structures for galactic perspective.
- **Spectral Color Toggle**: Switch between tier palette and astrophysical blackbody temperature colors (OBAFGKM).
- **Click to Inspect**: Click any binary star to open its animated 2-body Keplerian orbit inspector.
- **Camera Views**: Quick jump between **Solar Neighborhood View** and **Galactic Center (Sgr A\*)**.
